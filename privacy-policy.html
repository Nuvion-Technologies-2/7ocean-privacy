Important   This is a restricted admin application. It is not available for public download and is distributed exclusively to authorized personnel of 7Ocean Waterpark.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Nuvion Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the 7Ocean Waterpark Admin mobile application ("the App"). This App is designed exclusively for authorized administrators and staff members of 7Ocean Waterpark.

By using this App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

2. Application Scope & Distribution

2.1 Restricted Distribution

This application is not a publicly available app. It is distributed through private channels exclusively to:

  • Authorized administrators of 7Ocean Waterpark
  • Designated staff members with management responsibilities
  • Approved personnel granted access by the park management

2.2 No Public Signup

The App does not contain any public registration or signup functionality. New user accounts are created exclusively by existing administrators through authorized channels.

User credentials are provisioned internally by 7Ocean Waterpark management. There is no self-service account creation process available within the App.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Authentication Data

  • Username and password used for administrator login
  • Authentication tokens stored securely in the iOS Keychain
  • Session identifiers for maintaining active sessions

3.2 Operational Data

  • Ticket plans - plan names, pricing, validity, and member limits
  • Food inventory - counters, categories, items, and pricing
  • Revenue reports - aggregated sales and transaction data
  • Member information - names, mobile numbers, and membership status
  • Transaction records - payment methods, amounts, and timestamps

3.3 Device Information

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version and build number
  • Network connection type

4. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely for:

  • Authentication - Verifying your identity and maintaining secure sessions
  • Operations management - Managing ticket plans, food inventory, and park resources
  • Reporting - Generating revenue reports, sales analytics, and operational summaries
  • Communication - Sending operational notifications related to park management
  • Security - Detecting unauthorized access and protecting account integrity

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • AES-256 Encryption - All API communications are encrypted using AES-256-CBC encryption
  • iOS Keychain - Authentication tokens are stored in the secure iOS Keychain, protected by hardware encryption
  • Obfuscated Endpoints - API URLs and encryption secrets are obfuscated in the binary to prevent reverse engineering
  • Token-Based Auth - All API requests require valid authentication tokens
  • Secure Transport - All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local storage - Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device in the iOS Keychain
  • Server storage - Operational data is stored on secure servers managed by Nuvion Technologies
  • Session data - Cached data is cleared when you log out or when the session expires
  • Data retention - Operational data is retained as long as your administrator account is active

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • With 7Ocean Waterpark management - Operational data is accessible to authorized park administrators
  • Legal requirements - When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Security purposes - To protect the rights, property, or safety of Nuvion Technologies, 7Ocean Waterpark, or others

8. Your Responsibilities

As an authorized user of this App, you are responsible for:

  • Maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials
  • Not sharing your account access with unauthorized individuals
  • Reporting any suspicious activity or security breaches immediately
  • Logging out of the App when not in use
  • Ensuring your device has a passcode/biometric lock enabled

9. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for authorized adult administrators and staff members. It is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective when posted within the App or communicated through official channels. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
